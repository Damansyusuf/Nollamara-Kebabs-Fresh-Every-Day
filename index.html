Our Story

Where every dough is made from scratch

Nollamara Kebabs isn't just another takeaway. We bake fresh Turkish bread, pides, and burger buns every single day — because great food starts with great foundations.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch or settling in for a long dinner, our renovated, welcoming space and friendly team have you covered.

Dough made fresh daily, in-house Fully renovated, clean & welcoming space Indoor & outdoor seating Vegetarian-friendly menu options, everything is halal Free parking on site
